<Note>
  Teams need Sketa Plus and are managed by admins.
</Note>

A team is a group of workspace members. Restrict a shared collection to one or more teams and only their members, plus the admins, see it.

## Create a team

1. Open **Settings → Workspace → Members**, go to the **Teams** tab and click **New team**.
2. Enter a **Name** (unique in the workspace) and click **Create team**.
3. From the team's menu, **Edit members**, pick workspace members and **Save members**.

**Rename** and **Delete** are in the same menu. Each team shows its members and which collections it **Restricts**.

## Restrict a collection

When you create or edit a shared collection, set **Who can see it** to **Only these teams** and pick the teams. **Every member** opens it again. Admins always see restricted collections.

## What happens when…

- **Someone leaves a team**: they lose access to the collections restricted to it, unless another of their teams opens them.
- **A team is deleted**: collections restricted only to it stay restricted and become visible to admins only.
- **API keys and MCP connections** see what their member sees.
