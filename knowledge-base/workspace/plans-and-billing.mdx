## Plans

**Free** is for one person: the full editor, 1 cloud scene, live collaboration by link, PNG/SVG/file export, encrypted snapshot links, a library in your browser, compressed images and 10 AI units a day. You can also be a guest on scenes of Plus workspaces.

**Sketa Plus** is per workspace, billed per seat:

- **US$3 per seat/month**, or
- **US$30 per seat/year** (2 months free).

"Each member is a seat. Guests are free." Plus gives everyone in the workspace unlimited scenes and collections, members, guests, teams, live collaboration between members, public links and embeds, slides, comments, version history and trash, PDF and PPTX exports, images up to 4K, the server library, 100 AI units a day per member, teams, roles and invites, the audit log, the REST API and MCP. Voice and screen sharing are coming soon.

The **Billing** page compares both plans side by side, with every benefit listed.

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Admins open **Settings → Workspace → Billing** for the current workspace (or **Upgrade** anywhere in the app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick monthly or yearly">
    Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly** (2 months free). The Plus card shows the total: seats × price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade to Plus">
    **Upgrade to Plus** opens Stripe Checkout. Pay by credit card; have a promo code? Add it at checkout. There is no free trial.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After paying, Sketa waits for Stripe to confirm ("Confirming your subscription") and shows "Welcome to Sketa Plus!"

## Seats

The number of seats is the number of workspace members. Adding a member adds a seat and removing one takes it away, with prorated charges. Guests never count.

## Manage your subscription

Once subscribed, **Current plan** shows Sketa Plus with its status, the price per seat, your seats, the billing interval and the next charge (or the date Plus ends, or the date to pay by after a failed payment). **Manage billing** opens the Stripe portal to change the interval, update your card or cancel; **Payment method** shows the card on file. **Invoices** lists past invoices to download.

When a payment fails, Plus is about to end or the workspace is read-only, a notice at the top of the page explains what happens next, with **Update card** or **Keep Plus** to open the portal.

## Status

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | No subscription. 1 cloud scene, no members. |
| **Plus** | Active; renews automatically. |
| **Payment failed** | A payment failed. Update your card within 7 days to keep editing. |
| **Cancel scheduled** | Plus ends on the date shown. Nothing is deleted. |
| **Read-only** | Plus ended, or the 7 days passed without payment. |

## Read-only workspaces

When Plus ends, the workspace becomes **read-only**: everyone can still open, view and export everything (including PDF and PPTX), but nothing can be edited or created. **Nothing is deleted.** Public links keep opening. API keys and MCP connections can read but not write. Subscribe again to edit.
