Manage members in **Settings → General**. Inviting, removing and changing roles need an admin, and members need a Plus workspace. Each member is a paid seat: US$3 a month or US$30 a year. See [Workspaces, members and guests](/getting-started/workspaces) for roles and guests.

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite member">
    In **Members**, click **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="E-mail and role">
    Enter the **Email address** and pick the **Role**: **Admin** or **Member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invite">
    Click **Send invite**. Sketa e-mails an invitation that expires in 3 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invites are one by one, by e-mail. There are no reusable invite links. You cannot invite someone who is already a member, already has a pending invite, or whose e-mail is blocked.

**Pending invites** lists the invites not accepted yet; **Cancel invite** stops one from working.

## Accept an invite

The invited person signs in (or signs up) with the invited e-mail and accepts the invite, or declines it. Once they join, the subscription's seat count goes up.

## Change a role

Pick **Admin** or **Member** next to a member. The owner's role cannot change.

## Remove a member

**Remove member** asks you to type the member's e-mail to confirm. A removed member:

- loses access to the workspace and every team;
- loses their personal API keys and AI connections to the workspace;
- leaves their private collection with the workspace.

The owner cannot be removed. The seat count goes down.

### Remove and block

Tick **Also block this e-mail**, then **Remove and block**: that e-mail cannot be invited again, nor accept an old invite, until an admin unblocks it. The **Blocked** list shows each blocked e-mail and who blocked it; **Unblock** lifts the block (the person does not rejoin by themselves).

## Leave a workspace

In **Settings → Workspaces**, **Leave workspace** removes you from a workspace you do not own. You cannot leave your personal workspace.

## Delete a workspace

The owner can delete a workspace (not the personal one) under **Delete workspace** in **Settings → General**, by typing its name. Everything in it is deleted.
