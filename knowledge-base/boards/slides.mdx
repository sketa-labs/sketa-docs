<Note>
  Slides need Sketa Plus.
</Note>

In Sketa, slides are [sections](/knowledge-base/editor/sections) on the canvas, without a separate slide editor. Draw a section (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>), select it and turn on **Use as slide** in the selection toolbar. Sections from boards made before this setting existed are slides already.

## The slides panel

Open **Slides** from the editor's main menu: "Sections used as slides. Drag to change the order."

- Drag a slide, or use **Move up** / **Move down**, to change the order.
- **Hide from presentation** keeps the section on the canvas but leaves it out of presenting and exports. **Show in presentation** brings it back.
- **Presenter notes**: text only the presenter sees. Notes are never shown on public links.
- Untitled sections show as **Slide 1**, **Slide 2**…
- With [pages](/knowledge-base/editor/pages), the panel and presenting use the slides of the page you are on; the board's link presents every page's slides, page by page.
- **Presentation settings**, at the bottom of the panel, apply to the whole board: **Transition** (**None**, **Fade** or **Slide**) and **Autoplay**, with **Seconds per slide** from 3 to 120. Your own presenting and the board's link both play with them.

Slide order, notes, the hidden flag and the presentation settings are saved in the board, so they sync with everyone and go into the [version history](/knowledge-base/boards/version-history).

## Present

- **Present** (or **Present from this slide**) plays the slides full screen. Use **Previous slide** / **Next slide**, open **Notes** to see your presenter notes and what is **Up next**, and watch the **Time elapsed**.
- **Present live** presents to everyone in the board: others see "*name* is presenting" and can **Follow** along. Only one person presents at a time.

## Present from the board's link

There is no separate slides link: a board has [one link](/knowledge-base/boards/sharing-and-public-links), and its slides are presented from it.

- Anyone who opens a **Can view** link (or a **Can comment** link without an account) sees **Present** while the board has slides. It plays them full screen with the board's presentation settings: **Previous slide** / **Next slide**, the arrow keys, Space, or a tap on the slide; **Exit presentation** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to the board.
- **Download** in the presentation gets the slides as **PDF** or **PowerPoint (PPTX)**.
- In **Share**, **⋯** → **Copy presentation link** copies the board's link with `?present` at the end: it opens straight into the presentation. People who join through a comment or edit link land on the board presenting.

Hidden slides and presenter notes are never presented or downloaded through the link.

## Export

Export the frames to PDF or PPTX from the export dialog's **Frames (PDF, PPTX)** tab. See [Files and exports](/knowledge-base/editor/files-and-exports#frames-pdf-and-powerpoint).

AI assistants can build slides too, through the [MCP server](/ai/tools-reference#slides).
