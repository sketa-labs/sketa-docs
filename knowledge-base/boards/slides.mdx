<Note>
  Slides need Sketa Plus.
</Note>

In Sketa, slides are [sections](/knowledge-base/editor/sections) on the canvas, without a separate slide editor. Draw a section (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>), select it and turn on **Use as slide** in the selection toolbar. Sections from boards made before this setting existed are slides already.

## The slides panel

Open **Slides** from the editor's main menu: "Sections used as slides. Drag to change the order."

- Drag a slide, or use **Move up** / **Move down**, to change the order.
- **Hide from presentation** keeps the section on the canvas but leaves it out of presenting and exports. **Show in presentation** brings it back.
- **Presenter notes**: text only the presenter sees. Notes are never shown on public links.
- Untitled sections show as **Slide 1**, **Slide 2**…
- With [pages](/knowledge-base/editor/pages), the panel and presenting use the slides of the page you are on; slides links play every page's slides, page by page.

Slide order, notes and the hidden flag are saved in the board, so they sync with everyone and go into the [version history](/knowledge-base/boards/version-history).

## Present

- **Present** (or **Present from this slide**) plays the slides full screen. Use **Previous slide** / **Next slide**, open **Notes** to see your presenter notes and what is **Up next**, and watch the **Time elapsed**.
- **Present live** presents to everyone in the board: others see "*name* is presenting" and can **Follow** along. Only one person presents at a time.

## Share a slides link

In **Share** → **Links**, **Slides link** creates a link at `https://sketa.app/slides/...` that anyone can open without an account. Its options:

- **Transition**: **None**, **Fade** or **Slide**.
- **Autoplay**, with **Seconds per slide** from 3 to 120.
- **Turn off downloads**. Otherwise viewers can **Download** the slides as **PDF** or **PowerPoint (PPTX)**.

Hidden slides and presenter notes never reach a slides link.

## Export

Export the frames to PDF or PPTX from the export dialog's **Frames (PDF, PPTX)** tab. See [Files and exports](/knowledge-base/editor/files-and-exports#frames-pdf-and-powerpoint).

AI assistants can build slides too, through the [MCP server](/ai/tools-reference#slides).
