<Note>
  Voice conversations and Spotlight need Sketa Plus, on a cloud board in a workspace that is not read-only. They are not available on Free boards or live sessions by link. Everyone who can open the board's link can join the conversation, whatever its access and with or without an account, but only someone signed in can start one; a board embedded on another website has no conversation.
</Note>

## Join a conversation

Every cloud board on Plus has its conversation, always on. Anyone who can open the board can join it: members, guests with **Can view** included, and everyone who opens the board's link, with **Can view**, **Can comment** or **Can edit**, signed in or not. People without an account show with their random name, such as "Indigo Fox". Choose **Start conversation** (or **Join** when one is running) in the editor.

Only someone signed in can start a conversation. Without an account you join one that is already going (**Join conversation**, with the number of people in it); when nobody is talking, the button reads **Sign in to start a conversation** and opens the log-in and sign-up window, which brings you back to the board. Once you are in, you stay: the conversation goes on even after everyone signed in has left. Your browser asks for the microphone only when you join. Pick another one from **Microphone** if you need to.

A conversation takes up to 25 people. The 26th sees "This conversation is full" and keeps editing as usual. It is voice only: there is no camera and no screen sharing, and nothing is recorded.

## Mute and push-to-talk

- **Mute** and **Unmute** switch your microphone off and on without leaving.
- Hold the key left of <kbd>1</kbd> (the backquote key on most keyboards) to talk while it is pressed: "Hold ` to talk".

While someone speaks, a speaking indicator shows on their avatar and on their cursor.

Choose **Leave** to go out. The conversation ends for you if you lose access to the board; for people who came through the link, that includes the link being set to **No access**.

## Spotlight

People who can edit the board can choose **Spotlight me**. Everyone else in the board follows your view ("Following Ana"), and people who join later are pulled in too.

- **Stop following** lets a viewer move freely; **Follow** on the banner brings them back.
- **Stop** ends your Spotlight.
- Spotlight and live slide presenting share one slot: if someone else is presenting, "Someone else is presenting" and you wait for them to finish.
