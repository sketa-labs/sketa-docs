## Add a comment

Turn on **Comments** with the speech bubble in the toolbar or <kbd>C</kbd>, then click anywhere on the canvas: "Click anywhere to comment. Esc to stop." The comments panel opens with it. A comment on an element follows it when it moves, and stays where it was if the element is deleted.

- Type <kbd>@</kbd> to mention someone. Only people who can view the board can be mentioned.
- **Reply** to continue the thread.
- **Edit** or **Delete** your comments. Deleting the first comment deletes the whole thread.
- **Resolve** a thread when it is done; **Reopen** brings it back.

A board can have up to 500 open threads, and a comment up to 5,000 characters.

## The comments panel

The panel lists **Open** and **Resolved** threads. **Search comments** filters them; click one to jump to it on the canvas, on its own [page](/knowledge-base/editor/pages).

## Show or hide comments

<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd>, or **Show comments** in the main menu's **View** or the canvas menu, hides the comment pins (with a "Comments hidden" notice) and shows them again. It only changes your own view.

## Who can comment

Members who can open the board, and guests with **Can comment** or **Can edit**. Guests with **Can view** see comments but cannot add them.

The board's [link](/knowledge-base/boards/sharing-and-public-links#link-access) follows the same ladder, for everyone who opens it:

| Link access | Sees the board | Comments | Edits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **No access** | No | No | No |
| **Can view** | Yes | No, comments never show | No |
| **Can comment** | Yes | Reads and writes | No |
| **Can edit** | Yes | Reads and writes | Yes |

People who open a comment or edit link **without an account** comment too, under their random name (such as "Indigo Fox") or the name they typed. They can edit and delete only their own comments, from the same browser, and resolve threads. They see the names of the people who commented but not their profile photos, can mention people by name, and never get e-mails; the people they mention or reply to do. Their comments stay on the board when the link stops working, and workspace admins can delete them.

## Comment settings per board

**Comment settings** on a board:

- **Show comments**: off hides every comment on that board, also from people on its link.
- **Allow new comments**: off keeps the existing threads but stops new ones, also through its link.

## E-mail notifications

Sketa e-mails you when someone mentions you or replies to you, in your language. Turn it off with **Comments** in [Settings → Notifications](/knowledge-base/account/profile-and-preferences#notifications).
