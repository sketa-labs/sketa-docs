A cloud board's **Share** dialog is one screen: invite people at the top, see the **People with access** below, then set **Link access** for **Anyone with the link**. It needs Sketa Plus.

<Note>
  Without an account, **Share** asks you to create a free one first; your drawing is then saved as a cloud board. On a free account, **Share** starts a [live session](/knowledge-base/boards/collaboration#live-sessions-by-link-free) instead.
</Note>

## Guests

Members of the workspace already see every board in the collections they can open. To give someone outside the workspace access to one board, invite them as a guest:

1. Enter their **E-mail**.
2. Pick the **Access**: **Can view**, **Can comment** or **Can edit**.
3. Click **Invite**.

Guests need a free Sketa account; until they sign up with that e-mail they show as **Pending sign-up**. They find the board under **Shared with me**. Guests never take a seat, and they cannot be members of the same workspace.

**People with access** lists the board's **Owner** (the person who created it) and every guest. Change a guest's access from their menu or **Remove** them. Guests who joined through a comment or edit link show **via link**.

Members and guests can talk in the board's [voice conversation](/knowledge-base/boards/voice-and-spotlight), which is always available. People who open a view link, or edit without an account, never join a conversation.

## Public links

A board has one board link and one slides link at most.

| Link | Opens at | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Board link** | `https://sketa.app/l/...` and, while it is **Can view**, embedded at `https://sketa.app/embed/...` | The board, with the link's access |
| **Slides link** | `https://sketa.app/slides/...` | The frames as a presentation. See [Slides](/knowledge-base/boards/slides#share-a-slides-link). |

The **Anyone with the link** menu sets the board link: **No access** (no link, the default), **Can view**, **Can comment** or **Can edit**. Picking an access creates the link, or changes the one there is; the line under it says what the access means. Then:

- **Copy link** copies it.
- **Slides link** creates, copies, sets up or deletes the slides link.
- **⋯** → **Copy embed code**, while the link is **Can view**.

Viewers see new changes within about a minute.

### Link access

A board link's **Access** has the same choices as inviting a person:

- **Can view** (the default): anyone with the link sees the board, **without an account**: the board name, zoom and download, in their own light or dark theme. **Copy embed code** puts it on any website in an iframe.
- **Can comment**: someone without an account sees the board read-only, with **Sign in to comment**. Once signed in (or after creating a free account) they join the board as a guest who can comment.
- **Can edit**: anyone with the link edits the board right away, **no account needed**. They get a random name such as "Indigo Fox", which shows on their cursor and on the sticky notes and stamps they add, and you see them draw live. They cannot comment, see who else has access or join the voice conversation. **Sign in to use your name and join calls** turns them into a guest with their own name.

Signed-in people who open a comment or edit link join the board as guests with that access, so you work together with presence, cursors and the voice conversation. No e-mail invite needed. Someone who already has more access keeps it, and members of the workspace just open the board. Comment and edit links cannot be embedded.

Comment and edit links need Sketa Plus; without it, the link can only view. Setting the link back to **Can view** or to **No access** removes the guests who joined through it, and anyone editing without an account is stopped at once; guests invited by e-mail stay. If the workspace becomes read-only, people editing through a link can only look.

### What viewers never see

View links (and comment links opened without an account) show only the drawing. Comments, presenter notes, hidden slides, the collaborators and the workspace or collection names never leave Sketa. People editing through an edit link without an account see the board as any editor does, but never comments, e-mails or the workspace or collection names.

### Stopping a link

- Set **Anyone with the link** to **No access** to stop sharing it: the link is deleted and no longer opens, the guests who joined through it lose the board, and anyone editing through it without an account is stopped. Picking an access again makes a new link, with a new address; the old one never opens again.
- **Slides link** → **Delete slides link** stops the slides link the same way.
- Admins can turn off public links for the whole workspace with **Allow public links** in [workspace settings](/knowledge-base/workspace/settings). Existing links stop opening until it is turned on again, and the guests who joined through a link lose their boards.
- A link also stops opening when its board is in the trash, or when the workspace no longer has Plus (unless it is read-only).
