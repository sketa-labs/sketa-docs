A cloud board's **Share** dialog has two tabs: **People** (guests) and **Links** (public links). Both need Sketa Plus.

<Note>
  Without an account, **Share** offers a live session and a link to a copy instead. See [Drawing without an account](/getting-started/drawing-without-an-account).
</Note>

## Guests

Members of the workspace already see every board in the collections they can open. To give someone outside the workspace access to one board, invite them as a guest:

1. Enter their **E-mail**.
2. Pick the **Access**: **Can view**, **Can comment** or **Can edit**.
3. Click **Invite**.

Guests need a free Sketa account; until they sign up with that e-mail they show as **Pending sign-up**. They find the board under **Shared with me**. Guests never take a seat, and they cannot be members of the same workspace. Change their access or **Remove** them from the same tab.

## Public links

A public link lets anyone open the board **without an account**. A board can have up to 20 links, each with its own options, and you can turn each one off (**Link on**) without deleting it.

| Link | Opens at | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **View link** | `https://sketa.app/l/...` and, embedded, `https://sketa.app/embed/...` | The board, read-only |
| **Slides link** | `https://sketa.app/slides/...` | The frames as a presentation. See [Slides](/knowledge-base/boards/slides#share-a-slides-link). |

Click **Create link**, then **Copy link**, or **Copy embed code** to put the board on any website in an iframe. Viewers see new changes within about a minute.

### Link options

**Options** on a view link sets how the board looks to viewers:

- **Show the board name**.
- **Show zoom and download**.
- **Dark mode** and **Let viewers switch theme**.
- **Lock the view**: no panning, zooming or selecting.
- **Show frame outlines and names**.
- **Area shown**: **Whole board**, **Current selection**, a **Saved area** or one frame.

### What viewers never see

Public links show only the drawing. Comments, presenter notes, hidden slides, the collaborators and the workspace or collection names never leave Sketa.

### Turning links off

- **Public links for this board**, in the Links tab, turns off every link of that board. They are kept and come back when you turn it on again.
- Admins can turn off public links for the whole workspace with **Allow public links** in [workspace settings](/knowledge-base/workspace/settings). Existing links stop opening until it is turned on again.
- A link also stops opening when its board is in the trash, or when the workspace no longer has Plus (unless it is read-only).
