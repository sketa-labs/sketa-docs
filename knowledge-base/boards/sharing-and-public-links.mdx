A cloud board's **Share** dialog has two tabs: **People** (guests) and **Links** (public links). Both need Sketa Plus.

<Note>
  Without an account, **Share** asks you to create a free one first; your drawing is then saved as a cloud board. On a free account, **Share** starts a [live session](/knowledge-base/boards/collaboration#live-sessions-by-link-free) instead.
</Note>

## Guests

Members of the workspace already see every board in the collections they can open. To give someone outside the workspace access to one board, invite them as a guest:

1. Enter their **E-mail**.
2. Pick the **Access**: **Can view**, **Can comment** or **Can edit**.
3. Click **Invite**.

Guests need a free Sketa account; until they sign up with that e-mail they show as **Pending sign-up**. They find the board under **Shared with me**. Guests never take a seat, and they cannot be members of the same workspace. Change their access or **Remove** them from the same tab. Guests who joined through a comment or edit link show **via link**.

The **Voice conversations** switch, at the bottom of the People tab, turns [voice conversations](/knowledge-base/boards/voice-and-spotlight) on or off for the board: members and guests can talk on it. People who open a view link never join a conversation.

## Public links

A board can have up to 20 links, and you can turn each one off (**Link on**) without deleting it.

| Link | Opens at | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Board link** | `https://sketa.app/l/...` and, for view links, embedded at `https://sketa.app/embed/...` | The board, with the link's access |
| **Slides link** | `https://sketa.app/slides/...` | The frames as a presentation. See [Slides](/knowledge-base/boards/slides#share-a-slides-link). |

Click **Create link**, then **Copy link**. Viewers see new changes within about a minute.

### Link access

**Options** on a board link sets its **Access**, the same choices as inviting a person:

- **Can view** (the default): anyone with the link sees the board, **without an account**: the board name, zoom and download, in their own light or dark theme. **Copy embed code** puts it on any website in an iframe.
- **Can comment** and **Can edit**: the person who opens the link signs in (or creates a free account) and joins the board as a guest with that access, so you work together with presence, cursors and the voice conversation. No e-mail invite needed. Someone who already has more access keeps it, and members of the workspace just open the board. These links cannot be embedded.

Comment and edit links need Sketa Plus; without it, links can only view. Setting a link back to **Can view**, turning it off or deleting it removes the guests who joined through it; guests invited by e-mail stay.

### What viewers never see

View links show only the drawing. Comments, presenter notes, hidden slides, the collaborators and the workspace or collection names never leave Sketa.

### Turning links off

- **Public links for this board**, in the Links tab, turns off every link of that board. They are kept and come back when you turn it on again; the guests who joined through a comment or edit link lose the board.
- Admins can turn off public links for the whole workspace with **Allow public links** in [workspace settings](/knowledge-base/workspace/settings). Existing links stop opening until it is turned on again, and the guests who joined through a link lose their boards.
- A link also stops opening when its board is in the trash, or when the workspace no longer has Plus (unless it is read-only).
