Several people can edit the same canvas at once. Every element has its own version and a deterministic tie-break, so simultaneous edits merge without losing anything, deletions reach everyone, and undo only reverts your own changes.

## What you see

- Each person's cursor with their name and color, and who is idle.
- What the others have selected.
- The **Laser pointer** (<kbd>K</kbd>), visible to everyone.
- The avatars of everyone in the board. Click someone to **follow** their view; **Stop following** to look around on your own.

If your connection drops, your changes are kept and sent when you reconnect.

## Live sessions by link (free)

On a cloud board of a free account, click **Share** → **Start session** and send the **Session link** (`https://sketa.app/room/...`). Starting a session needs an account; joining one does not. People who join get a random name like **Indigo Fox** until they pick their own, and their changes are saved to your board **while you are in the session**. Reloading your board brings you back into it.

**End session** ends it for everyone (Sketa asks first): the people in it are sent back to their own boards, where they can keep a copy, and the link then shows **This session has ended**. See [Drawing without an account](/getting-started/drawing-without-an-account#join-a-live-session) for what guests see.

## Plus boards

In a Plus workspace, cloud boards are live for everyone who can open them: members with access to the collection, and guests with **Can edit**, are together as soon as they open the board. There is no session to start.

- The status shows **All changes saved**, **Saving…** or, offline, that changes are kept in this browser.
- People with **Can view** or **Can comment** see edits live but cannot change the drawing (**View only**).
- A read-only workspace (Plus ended) can be viewed but not edited.

Talk to the people in a board with [voice conversations and Spotlight](/knowledge-base/boards/voice-and-spotlight).
