Your account settings are in **Settings → Account**: **Profile**, **Preferences** and **Notifications**.

## Profile

The profile page has three tabs.

### Profile

**Profile photo**: **Upload** a PNG, JPEG or WebP image (up to 2 MB), then crop it to a square: drag the square to move it or a corner to resize it, rotate, flip or straighten the image, and **Save**. Sketa saves a 512 by 512 pixel photo and shows it in the sidebar, the members list, comments, the audit log and next to you in a live board. The trash button removes it; your initials show instead.

**Name** and **Email address**. If you change your e-mail, verify it from the link Sketa sends.

**Delete account** deletes your account and all of its data. Workspaces you own are deleted with it and their Plus subscriptions are canceled. Confirm with your password, or, if you only sign in with GitHub or Google, by typing your e-mail address.

### Password & login

**Update password**: your current password, then the new one twice. If you signed up with GitHub or Google and have no password yet, **Set a password** asks only for the new one.

**Connected accounts** shows whether GitHub and Google are linked to your account. Sketa supports e-mail and password, GitHub and Google sign-in; there is no two-factor authentication.

### Sessions

**Active sessions** lists the browsers signed in to your account, with the current one marked **Active now**. **Log out other devices** signs out every other session; this one stays.

Old links to **Settings → Security** open the **Password & login** tab.

## Preferences

Each preference saves as soon as you change it.

- **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
- **Language**: **English** or **Português** (Brazilian Portuguese). Sketa also e-mails you in this language. Before you sign in, Sketa follows your browser language or the language you pick on the sign-in page.
- **After signing in**: the **Dashboard** or your **Last board**.
- **Board order**: **Last edited**, **Date created** or **Name**.

Dates and times follow your browser's time zone and your language's clock format.

## Notifications

**Comments**: receive an e-mail when someone mentions you or replies to you. The **Manage e-mail preferences** link at the bottom of those e-mails opens this page.
