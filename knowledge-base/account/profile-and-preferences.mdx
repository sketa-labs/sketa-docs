Your account settings are in **Settings → Account**.

## Profile

**Name** and **Email address**. If you change your e-mail, verify it from the link Sketa sends.

**Delete account** deletes your account and all of its data after you enter your password.

## Security

**Update password**: your current password, then the new one twice. Sketa supports e-mail and password or GitHub sign-in; there is no two-factor authentication.

## Preferences

"Choose how Sketa looks and which language it speaks."

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
- **Language**: **English** or **Português** (Brazilian Portuguese). Sketa also e-mails you in this language. Before you sign in, Sketa follows your browser language or the language you pick on the sign-in page.
- **After signing in, open**: the **Dashboard** or your **Last scene**.
- **Sort scenes by**: **Last edited**, **Date created** or **Name**.
- **Comment e-mails**: "E-mail me when someone mentions me or replies to me."

Dates and times follow your browser's time zone and your language's clock format.
