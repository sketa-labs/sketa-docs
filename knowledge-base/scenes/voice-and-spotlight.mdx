<Note>
  Voice conversations and Spotlight need Sketa Plus, on a cloud scene in a workspace that is not read-only. They are not available on Free scenes, anonymous rooms or public links.
</Note>

## Join a conversation

Anyone who can open the scene can join its conversation, guests with **Can view** included. Choose **Start conversation** (or **Join** when one is running) in the editor. Your browser asks for the microphone only when you join. Pick another one from **Microphone** if you need to.

A conversation takes up to 25 people. The 26th sees "This conversation is full" and keeps editing as usual. It is voice only: there is no camera and no screen sharing, and nothing is recorded.

## Mute and push-to-talk

- **Mute** and **Unmute** switch your microphone off and on without leaving.
- Hold the key left of <kbd>1</kbd> (the backquote key on most keyboards) to talk while it is pressed: "Hold ` to talk".

While someone speaks, a speaking indicator shows on their avatar and on their cursor.

Choose **Leave** to go out. The conversation ends for you if you lose access to the scene.

## Spotlight

People who can edit the scene can choose **Spotlight me**. Everyone else in the scene follows your view ("Following Ana"), and people who join later are pulled in too.

- **Stop following** lets a viewer move freely; **Follow** on the banner brings them back.
- **Stop** ends your Spotlight.
- Spotlight and live slide presenting share one slot: if someone else is presenting, "Someone else is presenting" and you wait for them to finish.

## Turn voice off for a scene

In the **Share** dialog, **Links** tab, the **Voice conversations** switch ("Let people in this scene talk to each other.") turns conversations on or off for that scene. Turning it off ends the one in progress.
