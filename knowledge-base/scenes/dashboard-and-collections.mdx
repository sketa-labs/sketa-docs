The dashboard opens after you sign in (or your last scene, if you chose that in [Preferences](/knowledge-base/account/profile-and-preferences)). It shows the current workspace.

## Home

- **Pinned**: the scenes you pinned.
- **Recent**: scenes you created or opened.
- **New scene** creates a scene in the collection you are in.

Each scene shows a preview, its name, who made it and when it was last edited.

On the Free plan the dashboard shows how many of your **1 cloud scene** you use. Upgrade to Plus for unlimited scenes.

## Collections

Collections are folders with a name and an optional emoji. They need Sketa Plus.

- **Private** is your own collection. Only you see it; every member has one.
- Shared collections are visible to every member, unless an admin restricts them to [teams](/knowledge-base/workspace/teams) (**Who can see it** → **Only these teams**). Admins always see restricted collections.
- **New collection** creates one; **Edit** renames it or changes its emoji; drag a collection (or **Move up** / **Move down**) to change the order.
- **Delete** moves the collection and its scenes to the trash. Your private collection cannot be deleted.

Open a collection to see its scenes and **Sort** them by **Last edited**, **Date created** or **Name**.

## Scene actions

From a scene's menu (**Scene actions**):

- **Rename**. Untitled scenes get a name automatically from their content, with AI.
- **Move** to another collection.
- **Pin** / **Unpin**.
- **Duplicate** makes a **Copy of** the scene.
- **Share** invites [guests](/knowledge-base/scenes/sharing-and-public-links#guests).
- **Delete** moves it to the trash.

## Search

Type in the sidebar search to find scenes by name **and** by the text on their canvas, word by word, ignoring case and accents.

## Shared with me

**Shared with me** lists the scenes other workspaces shared with you as a guest, with your access (**Can view**, **Can comment** or **Can edit**).

## Trash

Deleted scenes and collections go to **Trash**, where you can **Restore** them or **Delete forever**. Items are deleted for good after 30 days. On the Free plan, deleting your scene deletes it right away.
