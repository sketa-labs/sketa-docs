## Add a comment

Turn on **Comments** in the editor, then click anywhere on the canvas: "Click anywhere to comment. Esc to stop." A comment on an element follows it when it moves, and stays where it was if the element is deleted.

- Type <kbd>@</kbd> to mention someone. Only people who can view the scene can be mentioned.
- **Reply** to continue the thread.
- **Edit** or **Delete** your comments. Deleting the first comment deletes the whole thread.
- **Resolve** a thread when it is done; **Reopen** brings it back.

A scene can have up to 500 open threads, and a comment up to 5,000 characters.

## The comments panel

The panel lists **Open** and **Resolved** threads. **Search comments** filters them; click one to jump to it on the canvas.

## Who can comment

Members who can open the scene, and guests with **Can comment** or **Can edit**. Guests with **Can view** see comments but cannot add them. Comments never appear on [public links](/knowledge-base/scenes/sharing-and-public-links).

## Comment settings per scene

**Comment settings** on a scene:

- **Show comments**: off hides every comment on that scene.
- **Allow new comments**: off keeps the existing threads but stops new ones.

## E-mail notifications

Sketa e-mails you when someone mentions you or replies to you, in your language. Turn it off with **Comments** in [Settings → Notifications](/knowledge-base/account/profile-and-preferences#notifications).
