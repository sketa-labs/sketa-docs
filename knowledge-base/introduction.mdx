## What is Sketa?

Sketa is an online whiteboard for teams: diagrams, sticky-note brainstorms, tables and sketches. Shapes are clean by default (straight lines, a sans-serif font) and switch to a hand-drawn style with one setting; every hand-drawn shape keeps its own random seed, so its wobbly outline looks the same every time and for everyone. The full editor is free and works without an account, on desktop, tablet and phone (touch and pen).

## How the app is organized

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Editor** | The canvas, at [sketa.app](https://sketa.app) for drawings in your browser or on any cloud scene. See [Editor basics](/knowledge-base/editor/basics). |
| **Dashboard** | The first page after you sign in: **Home** (pinned and recent scenes), **Shared with me**, **Collections**, search and **Trash**. See [Dashboard and collections](/knowledge-base/scenes/dashboard-and-collections). |
| **Settings** | **Account**: Profile, Preferences, Notifications. **Workspace**: General, Billing, Teams, Audit log, API keys, Workspaces. |

## Scenes

A **scene** is one whiteboard. Without an account it lives in your browser. Signed in, scenes are saved to the cloud in a workspace, synced continuously, and open from any device:

- **Free**: 1 cloud scene per personal workspace.
- **Sketa Plus**: unlimited scenes in collections, plus [public links and embeds](/knowledge-base/scenes/sharing-and-public-links), [comments](/knowledge-base/scenes/comments), [version history](/knowledge-base/scenes/version-history), [slides](/knowledge-base/scenes/slides), PDF and PPTX [exports](/knowledge-base/editor/files-and-exports) and the server [library](/knowledge-base/editor/library).

## Collaboration

Edits from several people merge without losing anything: each element has its own version, deletions propagate, and undo only reverts your own changes. Live sessions by link are free for everyone; in a Plus workspace, members who open the same scene are together automatically. See [Collaboration](/knowledge-base/scenes/collaboration).

## AI

Describe a diagram in plain words or turn a wireframe in a frame into a working HTML page. Untitled scenes get a name automatically. Free accounts get 10 AI credits a day, Plus members 100. See [AI](/knowledge-base/ai).

## Privacy

Cloud scenes, shared links and live sessions are encrypted at rest and in transit. They are not end-to-end encrypted: Sketa needs to read content to make previews, search, exports, AI, the API and MCP work, and to handle abuse reports.
