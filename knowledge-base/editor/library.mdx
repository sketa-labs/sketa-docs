The library keeps groups of elements you want to reuse: icons, components, stencils.

## Add and use items

- Select elements and choose **Add to library**, or open **Library** and click **Add selection**.
- Click an item to insert it, or drag it onto the canvas.
- **Search library** finds items by name.
- Select items to **Export selected** or **Remove selected**.

Images and web embeds cannot be added to the library.

## Where items are saved

The library panel has up to three tabs:

| Tab | Saved | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This browser** | In this browser | You, on this device. Available to everyone, even without an account. |
| **Personal** | In your account (Plus) | You, on any device. |
| **Workspace** | In the workspace (Plus) | Every member of the workspace. |

**Copy to personal** copies a workspace item to your personal library. An item can be up to 1 MB. The panel shows the newest 200 server items; search to find older ones.

## Public libraries

**Browse libraries** in the library panel opens **Public libraries**: free shape libraries shared by the community. It works for everyone, even without an account.

- **Search libraries** matches names, descriptions, authors and tags.
- Each card shows the first items, the author, the item count and the tags. Click the thumbnails to preview every item of the library.
- **Install** adds the library's items to your library: the **Personal** tab on a Plus board, otherwise **This browser**. Items you already have are skipped.

On Sketa Plus, AI assistants and scripts can search the catalog and place its shapes on your boards too, through the [REST API](/api-reference/introduction) (`GET /public-libraries`) and the [MCP server](/ai/tools-reference#public-library-catalog).

### Publish a library

Signed in, you can share your own items in the catalog:

1. In the library panel, select the items to share.
2. Click **Publish to the catalog** in the selection bar.
3. Fill in **Name**, an optional **Description** and up to 5 **Tags** (separate them with commas), then click **Send for review**.

The Sketa team reviews every library before it appears in the catalog. Once approved, anyone can install it, with your name shown as the author.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Items per library | 100 |
| Library size | 5 MB |
| Libraries waiting for review | 5 per person |
| Name / description | 80 / 500 characters |
| Tags | 5, up to 24 characters each |

Images cannot go in public libraries.

### Review e-mails

We e-mail you, in your language, when your library is approved and listed, when it is not approved, and if a listed library is later removed from the catalog for breaking our rules. A rejection or removal can include a note from the reviewer. Your items always stay in your own library, and copies other people already installed stay with them.

### My submissions

Signed in, **Browse libraries** shows two tabs: **Browse** and **My submissions**. **My submissions** lists every library you sent, newest first, with its status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **In review** | Waiting for the Sketa team. |
| **Listed** | In the catalog; anyone can install it. |
| **Not approved** | It did not enter the catalog. The reviewer's note, if any, shows below it. |
| **Removed** | It was taken out of the catalog. |

While a library is **In review**, you can **Withdraw** it: it leaves the review queue and no longer counts toward your 5 libraries waiting for review.

### Report a library

If a library in the catalog is illegal, abusive, spam or otherwise breaks our rules, click the flag (**Report**) on its card or in its preview. Pick a reason, add details and, optionally, your e-mail so we can follow up, then click **Send report**. Reporting works without an account. The Sketa team reviews every report and removes libraries that break the rules.

## Library files

- **Export library** downloads your items as a `.sketalib` file.
- **Import library file** (or drop a `.sketalib` file on the canvas) adds its items.
- Opening a library install link from an https site asks **Install library?** before adding its shapes.
