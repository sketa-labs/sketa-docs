The library keeps groups of elements you want to reuse: icons, components, stencils.

## Add and use items

- Select elements and choose **Add to library**, or open **Library** and click **Add selection**.
- Click an item to insert it, or drag it onto the canvas.
- **Search library** finds items by name.
- Select items to **Export selected** or **Remove selected**.

Images and web embeds cannot be added to the library.

## Where items are saved

The library panel has up to three tabs:

| Tab | Saved | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This browser** | In this browser | You, on this device. Available to everyone, even without an account. |
| **Personal** | In your account (Plus) | You, on any device. |
| **Workspace** | In the workspace (Plus) | Every member of the workspace. |

**Copy to personal** copies a workspace item to your personal library. An item can be up to 1 MB. The panel shows the newest 200 server items; search to find older ones.

## Library files

- **Export library** downloads your items as a `.sketalib` file.
- **Import library file** (or drop a `.sketalib` file on the canvas) adds its items.
- Opening a library install link from an https site asks **Install library?** before adding its shapes.
