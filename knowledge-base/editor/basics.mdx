The editor is the same everywhere: on [sketa.app](https://sketa.app) without an account, on a free cloud scene and in a Plus workspace.

## The canvas

- **Pan**: the **Hand** tool (<kbd>H</kbd>), <kbd>Space</kbd> + drag, a middle or right drag, or scroll.
- **Zoom**: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + scroll or pinch, **Zoom in** / **Zoom out**, **Zoom to fit** (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd>) and **Zoom to selection** (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>2</kbd>).
- **Grid** and **Snap to objects** (edges, centers and equal spacing) from the menu.
- **Theme**: light or dark. Dark mode inverts the drawing's colors on screen without changing the scene.
- **Zen mode** hides the interface; **View mode** lets you look around without editing.
- **Canvas background** sets the color behind the drawing.

## The hand-drawn look

Every shape is drawn with a sketchy outline. **Sloppiness** sets how loose it is: **Architect** (clean), **Artist** (the default) and **Cartoonist** (very loose). Small shapes get proportionally less wobble.

Text uses one of eight fonts. The properties panel shows the top three as buttons: **Hand-drawn** (the default), **Normal** and **Code**. **More fonts** opens the font picker with five more: **Display**, **Rounded**, **Handwriting**, **Classic** and **Monospace**.

The picker has **Search fonts** and lists the fonts **In this scene** above **All fonts**. Press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> to open it while the **Text** tool is active or text is selected (labels inside shapes and on arrows included).

## Styles

Select one or more elements and set, in the properties panel:

- **Stroke** and **Background** colors (palette, shades, hex code or the eyedropper).
- **Fill**: **Hachure**, **Cross-hatch**, **Solid** or **Zigzag**.
- **Stroke width** and **Stroke style** (solid, dashed, dotted).
- **Sloppiness**, **Edges** (sharp or round) and **Opacity**.

The last style you used becomes the default for the next element. **Copy styles** and **Paste styles** move a style between elements.

## Text

Type free text with the **Text** tool, or double-click a rectangle, diamond, ellipse or sticky note to write inside it: the text wraps and stays centered as the shape changes. Arrows take a label too. Set **Font**, **Font size**, **Text align** and **Vertical align** in the properties panel.

## Arrows and lines

- Arrows **bind** to shapes: move or resize the shape and the arrow follows. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> to draw without binding.
- Three arrow types: **Sharp**, **Curved** and **Elbow** (right-angle routing).
- **Arrowheads** at either end: arrow, bar, circle, triangle, diamond (filled or outline) and crow's foot for ER diagrams.
- Lines can have many points, edited one by one with **Edit points**; **Close or open shape** turns a line into a fillable polygon.
- Flowchart shortcuts: with a shape selected, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + an arrow key adds the next node already connected; <kbd>Alt</kbd> + an arrow key moves between nodes.

## Editing

- Select by click, <kbd>Shift</kbd> + click, a selection box or the **Lasso selection**. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + click selects inside a group.
- Move, resize, rotate, **Flip horizontal** / **Flip vertical**.
- **Group** / **Ungroup** (nested groups work), **Align** and **Distribute**, and the layer order (**Bring forward**, **Send to back**…).
- **Duplicate**, **Lock or unlock**, **Delete**, **Edit link** (a web, e-mail or object link on any element).
- **Switch shape type** with <kbd>Tab</kbd> keeps everything else.
- **Crop image**, and paste images, text, SVG or spreadsheet data. Pasted data can become a **Bar chart**, **Line chart** or **Radar chart**.
- **Undo** and **Redo** only revert your own changes, never a collaborator's.
- **Find on canvas** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>) searches texts and frames.
- The **Command palette** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>/</kbd>) runs any command or tool by name, and **Stats** shows the exact position and size of the selection.

## Frames

A frame is a named area that holds elements: they move with it and are clipped at its edge. Use frames to organize a scene, to export one area, and as [slides](/knowledge-base/scenes/slides). Select elements and use **Wrap in frame**, or draw one with the **Frame** tool (<kbd>F</kbd>).

## Embeds

The **Web embed** tool shows a live web page on the canvas: paste a link from YouTube, Vimeo, Figma or any website. Links to X posts show as a post card.

See [Tools and shortcuts](/knowledge-base/editor/tools-and-shortcuts) for every key.
