Pick **Mind map** under **More** in the [+ panel](/knowledge-base/editor/insert-panel): a central topic appears in the middle of the view, ready to type.

## Grow the map

With a topic selected, or while typing in it:

| Key | Adds |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | **Add topic below**: a child of this topic |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | **Add next topic**: a sibling of this topic |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> | A new line in the same topic |

Each new topic is connected to its parent and you can start typing right away. The map lays itself out again as it grows: the central topic's branches balance on its right and left, and each branch stacks its topics evenly.

## It is ordinary content

Topics are shapes and the lines between them are connectors, so you can restyle, move, copy or delete them like any other element. A copied branch keeps working as a mind map.

The [Mind map template](/knowledge-base/editor/templates) starts you with a filled-in example.
