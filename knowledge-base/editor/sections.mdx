A **Section** is a named area of the board that holds elements: they move with it and stay inside its edge. Use sections to organize a board, to export one area, and as [slides](/knowledge-base/boards/slides).

## Add a section

- Pick **Section** in the toolbar (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>, or <kbd>F</kbd>) and drag on the canvas.
- Or select elements and use **Wrap in section** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> on macOS): a new section is drawn around them. It is also the last button of the selection toolbar when several elements are selected.

New sections are named **Section 1**, **Section 2**… in your language. The name sits in a chip above the top-left corner: double-click it, or use **Rename** in the selection toolbar, to change it.

## The section toolbar

Select a section to see its controls in the [selection toolbar](/knowledge-base/editor/selection-toolbar):

| Control | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Color** | The section's fill |
| **Stroke** | Its outline |
| **Rename** | Edits the name chip |
| **Hide contents** / **Show contents** | Hides everything inside: the section stays, striped, and its contents are not drawn, selected, exported or shown on public links |
| **Lock** | **Lock section** keeps the section in place; **Lock contents** locks everything inside it |
| **Resize to fit** | Fits the section around its contents, with some room on every side |
| **Use as slide** | Makes the section a slide of the board's presentation |

Sections never rotate, so they show no rotation handle.

## More on a section

Right-click a section, or open **Object** in the main menu, for **Select contents**, **Release contents**, **Delete contents**, **Remove from section** (for elements inside one) and **Remove section** (deletes the section and keeps what was inside).

## Sections and slides

A section is only a slide once you turn on **Use as slide**: the slides panel, presenting and [slides links](/knowledge-base/boards/slides) show only those. Sections of boards made before this setting existed stay slides.
