The **+** at the end of the toolbar (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd>) opens **Insert**, the panel of things to put on the board. Search it ("Try typing “cats”") or browse its tabs: **All**, **Stickers**, **Icons**, **Templates**, **Libraries** and **More**. Click an item to place it in the middle of the view, or drag it onto the canvas. **Recents** keeps what you used last, in this browser.

## Stickers

Illustrated stickers in packs: **Reactions**, **Smileys**, **Hands**, **Animals and nature**, **Food and drink** and **Activities** (Fluent Emoji by Microsoft, MIT license). A sticker goes on the board as an image you can move, resize and rotate.

**Add your own** uploads images to reuse as stickers. They stay in this browser (they are not shared with your workspace); removing one from the panel keeps it on the boards that use it.

## Icons

Thousands of line icons (Tabler Icons, MIT license), searchable by name. An icon is a vector: recolor it from the [selection toolbar](/knowledge-base/editor/selection-toolbar) and resize it without blur.

## Templates

Ready-made boards to start from. See [Templates](/knowledge-base/editor/templates).

## Libraries

**Your library** opens the items you saved to reuse (see [Library](/knowledge-base/editor/library)). **Browse libraries** opens the free community catalog, and the tab lists its libraries to preview and install. <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>2</kbd> opens the panel right on this tab.

## More

| Item | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Media** | Upload an image |
| **Links** | Embed a site or video |
| **AI diagram** | Describe it, get a diagram (see [AI](/knowledge-base/ai)) |
| **Mermaid** | Paste Mermaid, get shapes |
| **Mind map** | Topics that branch with <kbd>Tab</kbd> (see [Mind maps](/knowledge-base/editor/mind-maps)) |
| **Code block** | Code with syntax colors (see [Code blocks](/knowledge-base/editor/code-blocks)) |

On a phone, the panel opens from the toolbar's **more** sheet.
