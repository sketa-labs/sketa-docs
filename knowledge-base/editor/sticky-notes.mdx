title: "Sticky notes"
description: "Brainstorm with sticky notes: colors, authors and adding the next note with one key."

---

Pick the sticky stack in the toolbar (<kbd>S</kbd>, or <kbd>N</kbd>) and click the canvas to add a note, or drag to give it a size. Start typing right away: the text starts at the note's top-left corner. Pick the stack again to open its tray of colors.

## Text size

Notes start at **Small**. Pick another size in the selection toolbar's size menu: **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, **Extra large** or **Huge**, or type a number. When the text does not fit, the note grows taller; the text never shrinks. Drag a corner to scale the note and its text together.

## Colors

Select notes and open **Color** in the [selection toolbar](/knowledge-base/editor/selection-toolbar) to pick a **Sticky color**: **White**, **Gray**, **Orange**, **Yellow** (the default), **Green**, **Teal**, **Blue**, **Violet**, **Pink** or **Red**. The color you pick last is the color of the next note.

## Authors

Each new note is signed with the name of the person who added it, shown small in the note's corner: your name when you are signed in, or the name you use in a live session. Select notes and toggle **Show author** in the selection toolbar to hide or show it.

Authors stay inside the board: [read-only links, embeds and slides](/knowledge-base/boards/sharing-and-public-links) never show them.

## Add the next note

With one note selected, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> on macOS) for **Add sticky note to the right**: a blank note of the same size and color appears next to it, ready to type. It works while you are typing in a note too, so you can write a row of ideas without touching the mouse.

## Stacks

Drag notes onto another note to pile them on it. With two or more notes selected (and nothing else), **Stack** in the selection toolbar piles them onto the bottom one and **Spread out** lays them out in a grid.

## AI

Select two or more notes and open **AI** in the selection toolbar to **Summarize** them in a new note or **Sort into sections** by theme. See [AI](/knowledge-base/ai#summarize-sort-and-rewrite).

## Connect notes

A selected note shows the [quick connect](/knowledge-base/editor/shapes-and-quick-connect#quick-connect) dots like any shape: click one to add a connected note, or drag it to another element to draw an arrow between them.
