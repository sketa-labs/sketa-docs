## Stamps

Pick **Stamp** (<kbd>E</kbd>) in the toolbar, choose one in the palette above the toolbar and click anywhere to place it: **Thumbs up**, **Thumbs down**, **Heart**, **Star**, **Question**, **Plus one**, **Done** or **Laugh**. The tool stays active, so you can stamp several ideas in a row; pick another tool when you are done.

A stamp is part of the scene: it stays, syncs to everyone and is undone, moved, exported and deleted like any element. It shows in its author's collaborator color, and hovering it shows who placed it (**Stamped by**). Use stamps to vote on sticky notes, then count them.

Read-only links, embeds and slides show the stamps without their authors' names. **View mode** cannot stamp.

## Emote reactions

<Note>Reactions and cursor chat work in a live session: a scene with other people in it, a live link, or a Plus scene opened by several members.</Note>

Hold <kbd>E</kbd> on the canvas to open the emote wheel at your cursor, move to an emote and let go: **Love**, **Like**, **Haha**, **Party**, **Fire**, **Applause**, **Wow** or **Hmm** floats up for everyone. A quick tap of <kbd>E</kbd> picks the **Stamp** tool. On touch screens, use the **React** button at the top right.

Reactions are never saved in the scene.

## Cursor chat

Press <kbd>/</kbd> on the canvas to type a short message next to your cursor (up to 100 characters). Everyone in the session sees it follow your cursor; it fades 4 seconds after your last keystroke and is never saved.

Guests with view-only access can react and chat too.

## High five

Move your cursor next to someone else's and shake it quickly from side to side: a high five appears between you, and everyone in the session sees who high-fived whom. Like reactions, it is never saved. Turn it off with **Shake cursor for high five** in the main menu's **Preferences**.
