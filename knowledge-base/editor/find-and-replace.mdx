**Find on canvas** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> on macOS) opens the find bar. Type to search texts, labels inside shapes and on arrows, sticky notes, table cells and section names. Matching ignores case and accents.

## Go through the results

- <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd>: the next result; <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>↑</kbd>: the previous one.
- **Find next** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>) and **Find previous** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd>) do the same from the canvas; with the bar closed, they open it.
- The view moves to each result. Search covers the page you are on.

## Replace

**Find and replace** (in the main menu's **Edit**, or the button in the find bar) opens the **Replace** row:

- **Replace** swaps the current result and moves to the next one (<kbd>Enter</kbd> in the replace field).
- **Replace all** swaps every result at once (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd>).

Bold, links and other [formatting](/knowledge-base/editor/text-formatting) around the replaced words stay. Each replace is one step you can undo. In **View mode** you can find, but not replace.
