A board can have several pages, each its own canvas. A board starts with one page; add more when a board gets crowded or you want separate stages of the same work in one place.

## The pages panel

Open **Pages** with the button next to the board name, top left.

- **Add page** adds a page at the end and takes you there.
- Click a page to show it. **Previous page** and **Next page** (<kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd>, or **View** in the main menu) step through them.
- **Page options** (the `⋯` beside a page) has **Rename**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. Deleting a page deletes everything on it, after you confirm; the last page cannot be deleted.
- Drag a page to reorder it, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> with it focused.

The panel also shows who is on each page in a live session.

## Move elements to another page

Select elements, right-click them and pick **Move to page**, then the page. Labels and everything inside a section go with them. Arrows never connect elements of two different pages: an arrow left behind lets go of what moved.

## What is per page

Selection, **Find on canvas**, comments and the slides panel work on the page you are on. Your collaborators' cursors show only when they are on the same page; following someone or [Spotlight](/knowledge-base/boards/voice-and-spotlight) takes you to their page. Undo takes you back to the page where the change happened.

## Outside the editor

- [Read-only links and embeds](/knowledge-base/boards/sharing-and-public-links) open on the first page and let viewers switch pages.
- [Slides links](/knowledge-base/boards/slides) play the slides of every page, page by page.
- Previews and link images show the first page.
- The [API](/api-reference/introduction) and the [MCP server](/ai/introduction) read and write pages, and every element names its page in `pageId`.
