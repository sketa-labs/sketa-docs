## The shape library

Click the shapes in the toolbar (<kbd>R</kbd> for a rectangle, <kbd>O</kbd> for an ellipse, <kbd>X</kbd> for a connector) to open the **Shapes** panel on the left, like FigJam's: **Recents**, **Connections**, **Basic** and **Flowchart**. Type in **Search for a shape** to find one by name (FigJam's names work too, like "manual operation"), pick it and draw it like a rectangle.

| Section | Shapes |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Diamond**, **Triangle**, **Downward-pointing triangle**, **Pentagon**, **Hexagon**, **Octagon**, **Plus**, **Star**, **Heart**, **Left arrow**, **Right arrow**, **Up arrow**, **Down arrow**, **Chevron**, **Speech bubble**, **Cloud** |
| Flowchart | **Right-leaning parallelogram**, **Left-leaning parallelogram**, **Cylinder (database)**, **Horizontal cylinder**, **Document**, **Multiple documents**, **Folder**, **File**, **Predefined process**, **Internal storage**, **Manual input**, **Trapezoid** (wide at the top, a manual operation), **Shield**, **Summing junction**, **Or** |

Every shape takes a label (double-click it, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> with it selected), a fill, a line style and arrows that bind to its outline, like the basic shapes.

## Switching shapes

Select shapes and open **Shapes** in the [selection toolbar](/knowledge-base/editor/selection-toolbar) to turn them into another shape. The label, colors, size and connected arrows stay. **Switch shape type** in **Actions** steps through rectangle, diamond and ellipse (or between line and arrow types).

## Clean or hand-drawn

New shapes are clean by default: straight lines (**Sloppiness**: **Architect**) and the **Normal** font. For a sketchy look, pick **Artist** or **Cartoonist** under **Line** → **Sloppiness**, and the **Hand-drawn** font under **Text**. See [Editor basics](/knowledge-base/editor/basics#clean-or-hand-drawn).

## Quick connect

Select one shape, sticky note, table, image or embed and a small dot appears outside each of its sides.

- **Click a dot** to connect: when another element faces that side, an arrow links them; otherwise a copy of the shape appears on that side, already connected, and you can start typing its label. A gray preview shows what a click will do while you hover the dot.
- **Drag a dot** to draw an arrow from that side; drop it on another element to bind both ends.

Quick connect uses the same placement as the flowchart keys: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + an arrow key (<kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS) adds the next node in that direction, and <kbd>Alt</kbd> + an arrow key moves between connected nodes.
