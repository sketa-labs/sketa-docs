## The shape library

Next to **Rectangle**, **Diamond** and **Ellipse**, the toolbar's **Shapes** menu holds the rest of the library. Type in **Search for a shape** to find one by name, pick it and draw it like a rectangle.

| Kind | Shapes |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | **Triangle**, **Inverted triangle**, **Pentagon**, **Hexagon**, **Octagon**, **Star**, **Plus**, **Heart** |
| Arrows | **Right arrow**, **Left arrow**, **Up arrow**, **Down arrow**, **Chevron** |
| Flowchart | **Parallelogram**, **Trapezoid**, **Cylinder (database)**, **Document**, **Predefined process**, **Manual input** |
| Other | **Speech bubble**, **Cloud** |

Every shape takes a label (double-click it, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> with it selected), a fill, a line style and arrows that bind to its outline, like the basic shapes.

## Switching shapes

Select shapes and open **Shapes** in the [selection toolbar](/knowledge-base/editor/selection-toolbar) to turn them into another shape. The label, colors, size and connected arrows stay.

You can also press <kbd>Tab</kbd> (**Switch shape type**) to step through rectangle, diamond and ellipse (or between line and arrow types), and <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> to go back.

## Clean or hand-drawn

New shapes are clean by default: straight lines (**Sloppiness**: **Architect**) and the **Normal** font. For a sketchy look, pick **Artist** or **Cartoonist** under **Line** → **Sloppiness**, and the **Hand-drawn** font under **Text**. See [Editor basics](/knowledge-base/editor/basics#clean-or-hand-drawn).

## Quick connect

Select one shape, sticky note, table, image or embed and a small dot appears outside each of its sides.

- **Click a dot** to connect: when another element faces that side, an arrow links them; otherwise a copy of the shape appears on that side, already connected, and you can start typing its label. A gray preview shows what a click will do while you hover the dot.
- **Drag a dot** to draw an arrow from that side; drop it on another element to bind both ends.

Quick connect uses the same placement as the flowchart keys: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + an arrow key (<kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS) adds the next node in that direction, and <kbd>Alt</kbd> + an arrow key moves between connected nodes.
