Templates are ready-made boards in your language. Open them with **Templates** at the top right of the editor, or from the **Templates** tab of the [+ panel](/knowledge-base/editor/insert-panel) (**Browse all templates**).

## The gallery

**Search templates**, or browse **All templates**, **Suggested**, **By activity** (**Meetings**, **Diagramming**, **Brainstorming**) and **By role** (**Designer**, **Developer**, **User researcher**, **Student & educator**). Pick one and **Use template**: it lands in the middle of your view as one step you can undo, and the view zooms out to show all of it.

| Template | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Brainstorm** | Collect ideas on stickies and pick the best |
| **Retrospective** | What went well, what to change, who acts |
| **Weekly meeting** | Agenda, updates, decisions and actions |
| **Flowchart** | Steps and decisions joined by connectors |
| **Mind map** | A central topic branching into ideas |
| **Kanban board** | To do, in progress, review and done |
| **User journey map** | Stages, actions, thoughts and feelings |
| **Team chart** | Who leads whom, at a glance |
| **Quarterly planning** | Goals, a three-month timeline and risks |
| **SWOT analysis** | Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats |

Everything in a template is ordinary content: edit, move or delete any part of it.

## Templates for new boards

With **Show templates for new files** on (**Preferences** in the main menu), every new board opens the gallery with **Blank board** as the first choice. It is on by default when you are signed in; on [sketa.app](https://sketa.app) without an account, turn it on yourself.
