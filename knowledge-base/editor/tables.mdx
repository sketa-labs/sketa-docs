Pick **Table** in the toolbar and click the canvas for a 3 × 3 table, or drag to choose its size. A table is one element: it moves, resizes, syncs and undoes as a whole, and arrows bind to its outline.

## Typing in cells

Double-click a cell to type in it. Rows grow to fit their text.

| Key | Moves to |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | The next cell; past the last cell, a new row |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> | The previous cell |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | The cell below; on the last row, it finishes editing |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Stays in the cell and starts a new line |
| Arrow keys at the edge of the text | The neighboring cell |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Stops editing |

Cells take [text formatting](/knowledge-base/editor/text-formatting) (bold, strikethrough, links and bulleted lists).

## Rows and columns

- Select the table and click a **+** handle at its edge for **Add row** or **Add column**.
- Drag a line between rows or columns to **Resize row** or **Resize column**.
- Select the table and open **Table actions** in the [selection toolbar](/knowledge-base/editor/selection-toolbar): **Insert row above**, **Insert row below**, **Insert column left**, **Insert column right**, **Delete row**, **Delete column**, **Header row** (a shaded, bold first row) and **Cell color**.

A table has at most 50 rows and 50 columns.
