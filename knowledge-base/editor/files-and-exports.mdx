## Sketa files

A `.sketa` file holds a whole scene: its elements and the images it uses.

- **Save to file** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>) and **Save as...** download one.
- **Open** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd>) loads a file and replaces the canvas; Sketa asks first and offers to save a copy.
- Drop a file on the canvas to replace it, or hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while dropping to add it to what is already there.
- **Reset canvas** clears everything.

## Images: PNG and SVG

**Export image...** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>) opens a preview where you choose:

- **Only selected** or the whole scene.
- **Background** on or off (transparent).
- **Dark mode**.
- **Embed scene**: saves the scene inside the PNG or SVG so you can open the image in Sketa and keep editing. It makes the file larger.
- **Scale** and **File name**.

Then download a **PNG** or **SVG**, or **Copy** it to the clipboard. **Copy as PNG** (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd>) and **Copy as SVG** skip the dialog.

An SVG carries the fonts its text uses inside the file, so it looks the same in any browser or design tool, even where those fonts are not installed.

PNG, SVG and `.sketa` exports are free for everyone.

## Frames: PDF and PowerPoint

<Note>
  Frame exports need Sketa Plus. They keep working while a workspace is read-only.
</Note>

In the export dialog, the **Frames (PDF, PPTX)** tab exports one page or slide per [section](/knowledge-base/editor/sections), in slide order. Pick the frames (**Select all** / **Select none**), then **PDF** or **PPTX** and **Download**. Sketa renders the frames on its servers; the dialog shows the progress and the download starts when the file is ready. Hidden slides are left out.

People who open a [slides link](/knowledge-base/scenes/slides#share-a-slides-link) can download PDF or PowerPoint too, unless the link turns downloads off.
