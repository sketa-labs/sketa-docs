The toolbar's pens draw freehand strokes. Each pen stays active after a stroke, so you can keep drawing.

| Pen | Key | Draws |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marker** | <kbd>P</kbd>, <kbd>X</kbd> or <kbd>7</kbd> | Smooth ink that follows your pen pressure |
| **Highlighter** | <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> | A wide, see-through stroke that tints what is under it without hiding it |
| **Washi tape** | <kbd>U</kbd> | A straight strip of tape from where you start to where you let go |

## Colors, widths and patterns

While a pen is active, the bar above the toolbar shows its **Pen colors** and widths. Each pen remembers its own color and width, so switching from the marker to the highlighter and back keeps both. Washi tape also has a pattern: **Stripes**, **Dots** or **Gingham**.

Once drawn, a stroke is an element like any other: select it to move it, change its color in the [selection toolbar](/knowledge-base/editor/selection-toolbar), or erase it with the **Eraser** (<kbd>E</kbd>).

## Draw to shape

**Draw to shape** (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>X</kbd>, in **More tools**) turns a scribble into the shape it recognizes: a rectangle, an ellipse, a diamond, a line or an arrow.
