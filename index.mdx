# Sketa documentation

Sketa is an infinite whiteboard for diagrams and sketches that look **drawn by hand**. Open [sketa.app](https://sketa.app) and start drawing: no account needed. Sign in to save your scenes to the cloud, and upgrade a workspace to **Sketa Plus** to work as a team.

## Free and Plus

| | Free | Sketa Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | US$0 | US$3 per seat/month or US$30 per seat/year |
| Editor | Everything | Everything |
| Scenes | 1 in your browser (no account) or 1 in the cloud (free account) | Unlimited, in collections |
| Live collaboration | By link, unlimited people | By link, plus automatic between workspace members |
| Exports | PNG, SVG and `.sketa` files | Also PDF and PPTX of your frames |
| Read-only links, embeds, slides, comments, version history | | ✓ |
| Library | In your browser | Also saved to your account and shared with the workspace |
| AI | 10 requests a day | 100 requests a day per member |
| REST API and MCP | | ✓ |

See [Plans and billing](/knowledge-base/workspace/plans-and-billing).
