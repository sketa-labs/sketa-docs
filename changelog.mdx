<Update label="October 2026" description="A new board experience">
  Sketa's editor has a new look and feel, with the toolbar, menus and keys of today's whiteboards, and keeps everything Sketa had.

  - **Toolbar and menus**: a new toolbar, the [Actions](/knowledge-base/editor/actions-and-menus) palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>), the main menu with **File**, **Edit**, **View**, **Object**, **Text** and **Arrange**, and [preferences](/knowledge-base/editor/actions-and-menus#preferences) that follow you.
  - **Keyboard shortcuts**: the standard whiteboard keys everywhere (<kbd>S</kbd> sticky note, <kbd>M</kbd> marker, <kbd>X</kbd> connector, <kbd>E</kbd> stamp, <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> section…). See [Tools and shortcuts](/knowledge-base/editor/tools-and-shortcuts).
  - **[Sections](/knowledge-base/editor/sections)**: named areas you can hide, lock, resize to fit and use as slides.
  - **[Pages](/knowledge-base/editor/pages)**: several canvases in one board.
  - **[Stickers, icons and more](/knowledge-base/editor/insert-panel)**: the **+** panel with stickers, icons, libraries, [templates](/knowledge-base/editor/templates), [mind maps](/knowledge-base/editor/mind-maps) and [code blocks](/knowledge-base/editor/code-blocks).
  - **Text**: italic, underline, numbered lists and sizes from Small to Huge. See [Text formatting](/knowledge-base/editor/text-formatting).
  - **[Sticky notes](/knowledge-base/editor/sticky-notes)**: sizes, text from the top-left, notes that grow with their text, stacks.
  - **Shapes**: more flowchart shapes, like **Internal storage**, **Summing junction**, **Or**, **Shield**, **Folder** and **File**.
  - **[Find and replace](/knowledge-base/editor/find-and-replace)**, **Select inverse**, **Paste to replace**, **Paste over selection**, **Tidy up** and rotation by 90° and 180°.
  - **AI on the board**: [summarize, sort and rewrite](/knowledge-base/ai#summarize-sort-and-rewrite) sticky notes and texts.
  - **[High five](/knowledge-base/editor/stamps-chat-and-high-fives#high-five)**: shake your cursor next to someone else's.
</Update>
