Before drawing, an assistant reads a guide with [`get-format-guide-tool`](/ai/tools-reference#get-format-guide-tool) (`topic`: `diagrams`, `presentations` or `freeform`). The guides keep what assistants draw consistent with what people draw in Sketa. This page summarizes them so you know what to expect, and what to ask for.

## Diagrams

For flowcharts, architecture and other boxes-and-arrows content, assistants prefer `create-diagram-tool` (automatic layout) and check the result with `take-screenshot-tool`. `edit-scene-content-tool` is for small diagrams or precise placement.

- **Layout**: a 20 px grid; shapes 160 × 80 by default, same size for the same role; 80 px between shapes in a row and 100 px between rows; one flow direction per diagram; new content goes beside what is already there.
- **Shapes**: rectangles for steps, services and screens; ellipses for start and end; diamonds for decisions (short labels); frames to group a part under a name; sticky notes for side comments.
- **Labels**: text goes inside shapes as their `label`, under about four words per line; arrows get labels only when the relation is not obvious.
- **Arrows**: both ends bound to shapes so they follow them; `line` for connections without direction; dashed for optional or asynchronous flows.
- **Colors**: at most three hues per diagram, chosen by meaning. Fills use shade 2, strokes shade 5:

| gray | `#e4e5e9` | `#3d414a` |
| red | `#ffcfcb` | `#d1352b` |
| violet | `#d6ccff` | `#5f43cf` |
| blue | `#b8dcff` | `#1d63bd` |
| teal | `#a7efd8` | `#0c8a66` |

## Presentations

A presentation is a scene whose frames are the slides.

- **Slides** are 1920 × 1080 frames created with `create-slide-tool`, which places each one after the last and returns its safe area and regions.
- **Safe area**: 5 % in from every edge (96 px left and right, 54 px top and bottom). The title takes the top 15 %; the body is the rest, split into left and right halves for two columns.
- Every element of a slide belongs to its frame, so it moves and exports with the slide.
- **Text**: titles on one line at font size 64 to 80; body at 32 to 40, at most six lines; the `normal` font for long text and `display` (a bold headline font) only for short titles. One idea per slide.
- **Notes and order**: `list-slides-tool` and `update-slide-tool` manage titles, speaker notes (up to 10,000 characters, never shown on the slide or public links), the hidden flag and position.
- **Look**: one or two hues, the same on every slide, titles aligned on the same line.

## Freeform

- **Fonts**: `fontFamily` takes a role: `hand` (hand-drawn, the default), `normal` (sans) and `code` (monospace), plus `display` (bold headline), `rounded` (rounded sans), `script` (handwriting), `classic` (Helvetica-like sans) and `mono` (a second monospace). One or two fonts per board; mix only for contrast, such as a `display` heading over `normal` text.
- **Notes and brainstorms**: one idea per sticky note (200 × 200), up to about 20 words, on a grid with 40 px gaps, themes grouped under named frames, one sticky color per theme or author.
- **Moodboards**: images from `add-image-tool`, 400 to 600 px wide, in rows with 40 px gaps, small captions underneath, framed so the board exports as one picture.
- **Wireframes**: clean lines (`roughness: 0`, stroke `#3d414a`, `normal` font); screens as 390 × 844 (phone) or 1440 × 900 (desktop) frames; buttons 120 × 40, inputs 280 × 40; arrows from the control that leads to the next screen.
- **Charts**: bars on a shared baseline with equal widths and gaps, values above and categories below, two lines for the axes, one hue with a stronger shade for the bar that matters.

## Prompts that work well

```
Read the diagrams guide, then draw our signup flow in a new scene called "Signup":
visitor lands, fills the form, confirms the e-mail, picks a plan. Screenshot it when done.
```

```
Create a 4-slide deck in the scene "Q3 review": a title slide, two slides of results
with a simple bar chart, and a next-steps slide. Put my talking points in the speaker notes.
```

```
Make a brainstorm board with sticky notes grouped in frames by theme, from these ideas: ...
```
