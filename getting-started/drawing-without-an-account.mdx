Sketa never asks you to sign up before drawing. Open [sketa.app](https://sketa.app) and the full editor is there.

## Your drawing stays in the browser

- The board is saved automatically in this browser and is still there when you come back.
- Several tabs of the same browser stay in sync.
- To keep a copy elsewhere, use **Save to file** in the menu: it downloads a `.sketa` file you can **Open** later, on any computer. See [Files and exports](/knowledge-base/editor/files-and-exports).

<Note>
  Clearing your browser data deletes the drawing. Save it to a file, or create an account to keep it in the cloud.
</Note>

## Share a copy by link

Click **Share** → **Create link**. Sketa uploads a copy of the drawing as it is now and gives you a link. Anyone who opens it gets that copy in their own editor; later changes on either side are not shared. Opening a shared link replaces what is on your canvas, so Sketa asks first.

## Draw together live

Click **Share** → **Start session**, type **Your name** (shown on your cursor) and send the **Session link**. Everyone who opens it draws on the same canvas in real time, no account needed:

- You see each person's cursor, name and selection.
- Click someone in the avatars to follow their view.
- If you go offline, your changes are kept and sent when you reconnect.
- **Stop session** leaves it: your drawing stays in your browser, but changes stop syncing.

See [Collaboration](/knowledge-base/boards/collaboration).

## Privacy and retention

Shared links and live sessions are encrypted at rest on Sketa's servers; the link itself carries no key. Sketa can read them when needed, for example to act on an abuse report. Links and sessions nobody opens or edits for 90 days are deleted.

To report a shared drawing, use [sketa.app/abuse](https://sketa.app/abuse) with its link.

## When you create an account

After you sign up, Sketa asks **Save this drawing to your account?** Pick **Save to my account** to turn it into your cloud board; the copy in the browser stays as it is. A free account holds **1 cloud board**. See [Workspaces, members and guests](/getting-started/workspaces).
