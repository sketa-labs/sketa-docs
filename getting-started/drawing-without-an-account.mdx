Sketa never asks you to sign up before drawing. Open [sketa.app](https://sketa.app) and the full editor is there. At the top right you find **Templates**, **Share** and **Log in** (on a phone, **Templates** is in the main menu).

## Your drawing stays in the browser

- The board is saved automatically in this browser and is still there when you come back.
- Several tabs of the same browser stay in sync.
- To keep a copy elsewhere, use **Save to file** in the menu: it downloads a `.sketa` file you can **Open** later, on any computer. See [Files and exports](/knowledge-base/editor/files-and-exports).

<Note>
  Clearing your browser data deletes the drawing. Save it to a file, or create an account to keep it in the cloud.
</Note>

After you have drawn a bit, Sketa suggests **Save this board, it's free**. It never blocks the canvas: keep drawing, or pick **Not now** and it stays away for a day.

## Sharing needs a free account

Click **Share** and Sketa asks you to **Create a free account to share this board** (or log in, with Google, GitHub or your e-mail). Right after, your drawing is saved to your account as a cloud board, it opens, and its share menu is ready: start a live session and send the link. See [Collaboration](/knowledge-base/boards/collaboration).

If you sign up with your e-mail, confirm it first; the drawing is saved as soon as you do, in the same browser.

## Join a live session

Anyone who gets a **Session link** (`https://sketa.app/room/...`) joins it without an account:

- You get a random name like **Indigo Fox**, shown on your avatar, your cursor and the sticky notes and stamps you add. Change it in **Share** → **Your name**.
- You see each person's cursor, name and selection, and can click someone in the avatars to follow their view.
- Reloading the page brings you back into the session.
- The session's drawing is not yours: joining never changes the board in your browser.

**Leave session** takes you back to your own board, exactly as you left it. Sketa then offers **Keep a copy** of the session's drawing; it asks first if your board already has something on it. When the owner ends the session, you see **The owner ended the session** and get the same choice. A link to a session that ended shows **This session has ended**.

## Privacy and retention

Live sessions are encrypted at rest on Sketa's servers; the link itself carries no key. Sketa can read them when needed, for example to act on an abuse report. Sessions nobody opens or edits for 90 days are deleted.

To report a live session, use [sketa.app/abuse](https://sketa.app/abuse) with its link.

## When you create an account

To bring your drawing into the account, sign up or log in from **Share** or **Save this board, it's free**: the drawing becomes a cloud board and leaves the browser. Signing up or logging in from **Log in** leaves it where it is: once you are signed in, [sketa.app](https://sketa.app) opens your dashboard (or your last board, if you pick that under **After signing in** in your preferences), and the drawing in the browser shows again only when you are signed out. A free account holds **1 cloud board**. See [Workspaces, members and guests](/getting-started/workspaces).
