<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sketa and draw">
    Go to [sketa.app](https://sketa.app). The canvas opens right away, no sign-up. Pick a tool from the toolbar (or press <kbd>R</kbd> for a rectangle, <kbd>A</kbd> for an arrow, <kbd>T</kbd> for text) and draw. Your drawing is saved in this browser as you go. See [Editor basics](/knowledge-base/editor/basics).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it with a free account">
    Click **Share** and create a free account (or log in). Your drawing is saved to the cloud and its share menu opens: **Start session** and send the link, and everyone draws together without an account. See [Drawing without an account](/getting-started/drawing-without-an-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Click **Create account** in the menu and sign up with your name, e-mail and password, or with GitHub. You get a personal workspace on the Free plan. Sketa offers to **Save to my account** the drawing you had in the browser: it becomes your free cloud board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade to Sketa Plus">
    Open **Settings → Billing** and subscribe, monthly ($3 per seat) or yearly ($24 per seat, $2 a month). Plus unlocks unlimited boards, collections, members, guests, public links, slides, comments, version history, PDF and PPTX exports, the API and MCP. See [Plans and billing](/knowledge-base/workspace/plans-and-billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    In **Settings → Workspace → Members**, click **Invite member**, enter an e-mail and pick **Admin** or **Member**. Each member is a seat. To share one board with someone outside the workspace for free, invite them as a guest from the board's **Share** dialog. See [Workspaces, members and guests](/getting-started/workspaces).
  </Step>
</Steps>
