# Instagram

TryPost publishes feed posts, reels and stories to Instagram **professional accounts** (Business or Creator). There are two ways to connect one:

| Connection | Identifier | Use it when |
|------------|------------|-------------|
| **Instagram** | `instagram` | Your Business or Creator account signs in with Instagram directly. Uses Instagram's own login. |
| **Instagram via Facebook** | `instagram-facebook` | Your Business or Creator account is linked to a Facebook page and you manage it through Facebook. Needed for **music on reels**. |

Both start from the single **Instagram** card in the **Connect a new channel** dialog, which shows the two options side by side: "How would you like to connect your Instagram account?" Both offer automatic posting and sent post metrics; only the Facebook option adds **Music on reels**.

<Note>
  You can connect as many Instagram accounts as you want in one workspace. The two connection methods lead to the same identity, so one Instagram account can only be connected one way. Connecting it the other way does not offer it again; to refresh its connection, use **Reconnect** on the existing channel instead, or [switch it to Facebook](#switch-an-account-to-facebook). See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting).
</Note>

## Connect your account

### Instagram (direct)

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel** (or use **New** > **Connect a new channel**)
2. Choose **Instagram** and click **Connect Instagram**
3. On the **Professional (Business & Creator)** card, click **Connect to Instagram**
4. Sign in on instagram.com and authorize TryPost. If your account is still personal, Instagram offers to convert it to a professional account
5. On the confirmation page, check the account and click **Finish connection**

### Instagram via Facebook

1. Open the **Instagram** card as above and use the **Professional (linked to a Facebook Page)** card. Open **Requirements** on that card to check them:
   - The Instagram account is **Business or Creator**, not a personal profile
   - It is **linked to a Facebook page**, not a Facebook profile
   - You are logged in as a **Page admin with full control**
   - You select **all permissions for all Pages and Instagram accounts** when Facebook asks, even ones you won't connect
2. Click **Connect through Facebook** and authorize TryPost
3. On the **Select accounts** page, tick the Instagram accounts you want and click **Finish connection**

Either way, TryPost opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks how many times a week you want to post.

### Switch an account to Facebook

An account connected with Instagram's own login can move to the Facebook connection without becoming a new channel. In **Settings → Channels**, open the channel's menu and choose **Link to a Facebook Page**, then connect through Facebook as above. The channel stays the same, now connected through Facebook, and keeps its scheduled posts, queue and settings. Sign in with the Facebook Page linked to that same Instagram account; another account is refused and the channel is left as it was.

The composer offers the same switch when you try to add music on an account that is not connected through Facebook (see [Music](#music)).

Moving the other way (a Facebook connection reconnected with Instagram's own login) also keeps the channel, but reels with music can no longer publish there. A scheduled reel with music then fails with "Music needs an Instagram account connected through Facebook." Remove the music, or connect the account through Facebook again, then retry it.

## Supported content types

| Type | `content_type` | Media | Description |
|------|----------------|-------|-------------|
| **Feed Post** | `instagram_feed` | Up to 10 images and videos (required) | Single image, a carousel of up to 10 images and videos, or a single video (published as a Reel) |
| **Reel** | `instagram_reel` | 1 video (required) | Short-form vertical video |
| **Story** | `instagram_story` | 1 image or video (required) | Visible for 24 hours. Stories carry no caption, so the post text is not sent |

There is no separate carousel content type: a multi-image post is an `instagram_feed`. All three types work on both connection methods.

## Media limits

| `content_type` | Image | Video | Duration | Aspect ratio | Format |
|----------------|-------|-------|----------|--------------|--------|
| `instagram_feed` | 8 MB | 100 MB | 60 seconds | Images 3:4 to 1.91:1 | JPEG after upload. Video MP4 or MOV |
| `instagram_reel` | — | 300 MB | 15 minutes | — | MOV or MP4 |
| `instagram_story` | 8 MB | 100 MB | 60 seconds | Images fitted to 9:16 | Same as feed |

A feed image outside 3:4 to 1.91:1 blocks scheduling and publishing; use the crop presets (3:4, 4:5, 1:1, 1.91:1) in **Edit Media**. GIFs are not accepted. A single video on `instagram_feed` publishes through the Reels container; the 100 MB / 60 seconds on `instagram_feed` is TryPost's conservative rule (Meta only documents the Reel spec) — use `instagram_reel` for longer clips. Official specs: [IG User Media](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media/). Full catalog: [Media](/knowledge-base/media#instagram).

## Text and per-post options

- **2,200 characters** per caption.
- **5 hashtags at most.** A post with more cannot be scheduled or published (web, API and MCP); a draft can still be saved. Stories are exempt because they send no caption.
- **Share to feed** — a Reel also appears on your profile grid by default; turn it off with **Share to feed** (`meta.share_to_feed`).
- **AI-generated** — label the post as made with AI (`meta.is_ai_generated`).
- **Music** — add a track from Instagram's library to a Reel (accounts connected through Facebook). See [Music](#music).
- **Collaborators** — invite up to 3 accounts as co-authors of a feed post, carousel or Reel. See [Collaborators](#collaborators).
- **Trial reel** — show a Reel to non-followers first. See [Trial reels](#trial-reels).
- **Music**, **Trial reel** and **Share to feed** apply to the **Reel** type only. A **Feed Post** with a single video still publishes as a Reel, but without them.
- **Tag people** — tag up to 20 accounts on each feed image in **Edit Media** > **Tag People** (`media[].meta.user_tags`).
- **Video cover** — pick the frame shown as the cover of a feed or reel video in **Edit Media** > **Thumbnail**.
- **Alt text** — up to 1,000 characters per image.

### Music

**Music** appears on a **Reel** when the account is connected through Facebook (`instagram-facebook`). Instagram offers its audio library only on that connection.

1. Click **Add music**. The list starts with what is trending; type in **Search music** to find a track. Results include licensed music and original sounds, and only tracks whose title or artist contains every word you typed. "Results are limited and may differ from Instagram."
2. Use **Play preview** to listen, then pick a track.
3. Set the **Volume** of the **Music** and of the **Video** (the reel's own sound), from 0 to 100. Both start at 100.

"Instagram doesn't let TryPost choose which part of a track plays." **Remove music** takes the track off.

A workspace can make 30 music searches a minute that are not already cached; searches you repeat within ten minutes are answered from the cache and do not count. Past the limit, TryPost shows "Too many music searches. Wait a minute and try again."

On an account connected with Instagram's own login, **Add music** shows "Connect through Facebook to publish reels with music". **Save & link** saves your post and opens the Facebook connection for that channel: a scheduled post stays scheduled, anything else is saved as a draft. When you come back, reopen the post to add the music. **Maybe later** closes it.

In the API and MCP the track is `meta.audio`: its `id` from [Search Instagram audio](/api-reference/social-accounts/search-instagram-audio) (a string of digits), and `audio_volume` and `video_volume` from 0 to 100. Copy the result's `title`, `artist`, `cover_url` and `preview_url` too, so the composer shows the track; when they are missing, it fills them in the first time you open the post.

### Collaborators

**Collaborators** invites up to 3 Instagram accounts as co-authors of a **Feed Post** (single image, video or carousel) or a **Reel**. Stories take no collaborators. Type each username (with or without `@`) and click **Add collaborator**.

Each invited account gets an invite in the Instagram app and must accept it before the post shows on their profile too. "Instagram can't confirm a username for us. Open one to check it's the right account." A username that does not exist makes the post fail when it publishes.

In the API and MCP: `meta.collaborators`, an array of up to 3 usernames. TryPost stores them without `@` and without duplicates.

### Trial reels

**Trial reel** on a **Reel** shows it to non-followers first:

| Option | `meta.trial_graduation` | What happens |
|--------|-------------------------|--------------|
| **Off** | `null` (omit it) | A normal Reel. |
| **Auto** | `SS_PERFORMANCE` | "Your trial reel is shown to non-followers first. If it performs well, Instagram shares it with your followers automatically after 72 hours. If it doesn't, it won't be shared unless you share it yourself." |
| **Manual** | `MANUAL` | "Non-followers see it first. You share it with your followers when you're ready." |

Trial reels only work on accounts where Instagram offers them. Before you turn one on, TryPost asks: "Are trial reels available when you create a reel in the Instagram app?" Check in the Instagram mobile app first.

A trial reel cannot have collaborators: with collaborators set the option shows "Not available with collaborators", and the other way around.

## Insights

Instagram metrics, followers and follows appear in [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview), for both connection methods. Instagram channels also get a **Grid** view on their Publish page that approximates your profile grid.

Either connection can be a [Repurpose](/knowledge-base/repurpose) source: TryPost watches the account for new Reels, feed videos or Stories you publish outside TryPost and republishes them to the channels you pick.

## OAuth scopes

| Connection | Scopes requested |
|------------|------------------|
| Instagram (`instagram`) | `instagram_business_basic`, `instagram_business_content_publish`, `instagram_business_manage_insights` |
| Instagram via Facebook (`instagram-facebook`) | `public_profile`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `business_management`, `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_insights` |

The `*_manage_insights` scopes power Instagram metrics in Insights.

## Self-hosting setup

<Accordion title="Configure Instagram API credentials">
  1. Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/) and create an app
  2. Add the **Instagram** product (Instagram API with Instagram Login)
  3. Set the redirect URL to `{APP_URL}/accounts/instagram/callback`
  4. Request permissions: `instagram_business_basic`, `instagram_business_content_publish`, `instagram_business_manage_insights`
  5. Add to your `.env`:

  ```env
  INSTAGRAM_CLIENT_ID=your_app_id
  INSTAGRAM_CLIENT_SECRET=your_app_secret
  INSTAGRAM_CLIENT_REDIRECT="${APP_URL}/accounts/instagram/callback"
  ```
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Configure Instagram via Facebook API credentials">
  1. In the same Meta for Developers app, also add the **Facebook Login for Business** product
  2. Set the redirect URL to `{APP_URL}/accounts/instagram-facebook/callback` (fixed by the app route; there is no env variable for it)
  3. Request permissions: `public_profile`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `business_management`, `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_insights`
  4. Use the same `FACEBOOK_CLIENT_ID` / `FACEBOOK_CLIENT_SECRET` you've configured for [Facebook](/platforms/facebook#self-hosting-setup)
</Accordion>

Turn either method off with `INSTAGRAM_ENABLED=false` or `INSTAGRAM_FACEBOOK_ENABLED=false`; the Instagram card shows while at least one is on.
