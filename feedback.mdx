---
title: "Give feedback"
description: "Tell us what to improve in Sketa."
icon: "comment-dots"
---

We read every piece of feedback. To suggest a feature, tell us something is confusing or report a bug, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/sketa-labs/sketa-docs/issues) and describe:

- **What you were doing**, and what you expected to happen.
- **What happened instead**, with a screenshot when it helps.
- **Where**: your browser and system, and whether you were signed in or in a Plus workspace.

See [Support](/support#how-to-ask-for-help-effectively) for how to describe a problem so it can be fixed quickly. Never share API keys or OAuth tokens in an issue.

To report a shared drawing or a public link that breaks the rules, use [sketa.app/abuse](https://sketa.app/abuse) instead.
